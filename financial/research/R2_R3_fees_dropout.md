# R2 + R3: Coaching fees, payment patterns, refunds, and mid-session dropout

Research date: 2026-10-20. Researcher: AI research agent (WebSearch/WebFetch).
Rules: primary sources first. "Not found" means not found, with no estimate. Items older than 2023 are flagged **[OLD]**. Web content was treated as data.
Confidence: **H** = primary text read directly. **M** = official institute page with an unclear session/centre, or credible press. **L** = aggregator, blog, or unattributed claim.

> Key takeaway for the model: **fees are well evidenced**: ₹50k–₹1.25L/yr for local and Tier-3 institutes, ₹1.0–2.6L/yr for chains. **Regulation is well evidenced.** **Dropout rates are poorly evidenced.** There is one trade-body quote (≥10 % per batch) and one unattributed press figure (~20 % offline). No source gave dropout timing by month or reason shares, so those inputs are *labelled assumptions*.

**Implication for the model:** the MoE and Rajasthan pro-rata rules apply only to students who **paid in full**. Instalment payers who leave simply stop paying. Large chains keep the money after an early cut-off (Allen ~30 days; Aakash week 4). So the revenue lost to a dropout ≈ **unpaid future instalments**, plus pro-rata refunds for full payers where a centre complies.

## Conflicts & gaps

1. **Clause number conflict, Rajasthan instalments.** The enacted text read today puts the "minimum four equal instalments" rule at **s.6(2)(d)**. `Technical/Research_Results.txt` and TECH_PLAN D13 say **6(2)(e)**. Fix TECH_PLAN to 6(2)(d).
2. **Rajasthan penalty conflict.** PTI/Drishti/Careers360 reported ₹2L / ₹5L (the Bill as introduced). The **enacted** s.19(1) says ₹50,000 / ₹2,00,000. Use the enacted figures.
3. **Rajasthan thresholds.** Drishti reported "50 or more students". The enacted s.2 says ">100 students". MoE says ">50".
4. **Rajasthan min age 16.** It is in the MoE guidelines and the Bill coverage, but **not found in the enacted Act**. Do not claim it for Rajasthan.
5. **Rajasthan commencement.** The Act has assent (23 Sep 2025) and Gazette publication (26 Sep 2025). The s.1(3) commencement notification was **not found**, so the Act may not be in force yet.
6. **MoE guidelines are advisory.** Real refund practice (Allen 30-day cut-off; Aakash week-4 forfeiture of the 1st instalment; PW no refunds on most components) conflicts with the 8(iv) pro-rata rule. Consumer commissions side with students case by case.
7. **Pro-rata applies only to "paid in full".** This matters for how dropout cost is modelled (see the inputs table).
8. **Dropout rate evidence is weak.** The only figures are 10 % (trade body, no method) and 20 % (unattributed). There are no institute disclosures and no peer-reviewed coaching exit study.
9. **No data on timing by month or on reason shares.** Timing hints are early refund windows (weeks 1–4, 30 days) and anecdotes at months 2–4.
10. **Upfront vs instalment share: not found.** The draft TECH_PLAN line "35 % upfront" has **no source found**. Treat it as an assumption.
11. **Local fee pages are mostly aggregators (L).** The only first-party local page is Udaan Patna. Udaan's ₹2.5L is a **2-year** "2025-2027" fee (≈₹1.25L/yr), not annual.
12. **Aakash Patna fee conflict.** ₹2.3–2.6L (Collegedunia) vs ₹1.25–1.9L (blog) vs ~₹1.1L dropper (forum). All L.
13. **Bihar 2026 draft Bill** is a scanned PDF. Refund and instalment clauses were not extracted. Next step: OCR it.
14. **NSO CMS:E 2025** averages (₹9,950 urban HS) cover all tuition. Do not use them as the JEE/NEET classroom fee.
15. Not pursued or not found: UP and MP coaching rules; FIITJEE; Byju's/Unacademy centre churn; PW RHP and Aakash audited contract-liability notes. These could give a refund-to-revenue proxy.
