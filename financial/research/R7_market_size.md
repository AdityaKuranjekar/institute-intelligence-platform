# R7 Market size (SAM) for independent JEE/NEET coaching institutes, 150-2,000 students

Research date: 2026-10-22. Method: WebSearch/WebFetch. Figures marked "search summary" come from search-result summaries and were not all opened at the source page; re-check the URL before putting them on a slide. "not found" means exactly that. Web content treated as data.

Headline: no primary source gives a count of coaching institutes by size anywhere in India. The SAM below is therefore a DERIVED range built on stated assumptions, cross-checked against one district-level registry number (Patna, 574). Treat it as a cap, not a forecast.

## (b) Derivation of serviceable addressable market (SAM)

Scope: independent institutes with 150-2,000 students, in Bihar + Uttar Pradesh (the beachhead states). A separate extension covers Rajasthan, MP and Jharkhand. Method is bottom-up from demand because no supply-side count exists.

Formula: SAM = (unique JEE+NEET aspirants x state share x years-in-pipeline x classroom-coaching share x foundation multiplier x independent share x share in 150+ institutes) / average students per qualifying institute.

| Step | Bear | Base | Bull | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Unique JEE+NEET aspirants per year, national (lakh) | 28 | 30 | 33 | DERIVED/ASSUMPTION. NEET 22.75 lakh registered + JEE Main unique 14-15.7 lakh = about 37-38 lakh gross; assumed 25-35% overlap between the two (no source). |
| 2. Bihar + UP share of aspirants | 17% | 19% | 21% | ASSUMPTION. Roughly their share of population (about 17%); no NTA state split found. |
| 3. Aspirants in Bihar+UP per year (step1 x step2) | 4.76 lakh | 5.70 lakh | 6.93 lakh | arithmetic |
| 4. Years a student stays in the pipeline (Class 11, 12, dropper) | 1.8 | 2.0 | 2.2 | ASSUMPTION |
| 5. Share taking classroom (offline) coaching | 30% | 45% | 55% | ASSUMPTION. Anchor: NSO all-student rate 27.0%, urban 30.7%; JEE/NEET aspirants are far above average, no figure found. |
| 6. Foundation/board students added (multiplier) | 1.2 | 1.5 | 1.8 | ASSUMPTION. Institutes of 150-2,000 also enrol Class 9-10 and board students, who count toward their size. |
| 7. Independent share (not Allen, Aakash, PW, etc.) | 60% | 70% | 80% | ASSUMPTION. Chains growing in Bihar Tier-2 (Allen). |
| 8. Share of those students in institutes with 150+ students | 45% | 55% | 65% | ASSUMPTION, no source. |
| Students in qualifying institutes (3x4x5x6x7x8) | 83,000 | 296,000 | 785,000 | arithmetic (checked with Python) |
| 9. Average enrolment per qualifying institute | 500 | 400 | 300 | ASSUMPTION |
| **SAM, Bihar + UP (institutes)** | **about 170** | **about 740** | **about 2,600** | arithmetic |

Cross-check against the only hard number (Patna district, 574 registered, all subjects): if 20-25% of Patna's registered institutes are independent, JEE/NEET-relevant and 150+ students, Patna holds about 115-145. If Patna is 10-15% of Bihar's qualifying institutes (hub effect, assumption), Bihar is about 800-1,400, which sits at the upper end of the Base figure for Bihar+UP as a whole. This hints the Base may be conservative on the Patna side, or that Patna is a larger share of Bihar than assumed. Both are assumptions; the cross-check does not validate the Base, it only shows it is not absurd.

Extension (assumption): Rajasthan + MP + Jharkhand add roughly +40% / +60% / +80% (Bear/Base/Bull) to the Bihar+UP figure, with Kota counted mostly as chain territory. Extended SAM about 240 / 1,180 / 4,700.

What the number does not capture: online-only institutes, institutes under 150 students (excluded by our price-vs-value logic), and any growth in aspirants after 2026.

## (c) Conflicts and gaps

1. No source for institutes by size, anywhere. All size steps are assumptions.
2. No statewide registered count for Bihar, UP, Rajasthan, MP, Jharkhand. UP has no consolidated list and has just ordered a district survey (June 2026): check that output when published, it could replace the bottom-up.
3. NEET registrations: 22.75 lakh (Deccan Herald) vs "nearly 25 lakh" (Careers360) vs 22.79 lakh. The 2026 cycle was disrupted by the 3 May cancellation and 21 June re-exam; 2026 may not be a typical year. Use 2025 NTA data as a second year before the deck.
4. JEE Main unique candidates: 15.66 lakh vs 14.10 + 3 lakh vs "17 lakh" (sum of sessions). Not NTA primary pages; pull NTA's own press note.
5. Market size: USD 244 m (IMARC narrow) vs USD 7.2 bn vs 11.6 bn vs 23-26 bn (FY30 forecast). Rupee JEE/NEET-offline size: not found. Recommend not putting a TAM rupee figure on a slide unless the Redseer report itself is read.
6. Patna "6,000 operating" and "3,000 unregistered" are local-press claims, unverified.
7. Rajasthan Act threshold (50 vs 100 students) and enactment status conflict in these results; the project's own primary reading (Act 16/2025) overrides.
8. Coaching participation for higher secondary or JEE/NEET specifically: not found; open the NSS Report 595 tables.
9. Older than 2023: Classplus and Teachmint figures (2020). Flagged.
10. Search-summary figures were not opened at the source page for several rows; re-verify before slide use.
